# Exogenous empty supply \(\xi\)

Empty supply \(\xi_{n,t}\) is **deterministic and exogenous**. Do **not** invent laden→empty conversion dynamics. Spokes may also receive exogenous top-up \(E_{n,t}\). Initial inventory \(I_{n,0}\) and storage caps \(U_n\) are given in data.
