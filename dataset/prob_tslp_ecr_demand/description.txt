A liner operator must reposition empty containers under **export-demand uncertainty** on a sea–land network with hub seaports, spoke seaports, and dry ports (no shipper/consignee nodes). Empty supply \(\xi_{n,t}\) is an **exogenous deterministic** parameter. Randomness is confined to scenario-dependent export demand \(\eta_{n,t}^\omega\).

Formulate and solve a **single planning-window two-stage stochastic linear program** [D1]–[D2] (deterministic equivalent / DEP). All decision variables are continuous nonnegative TEUs.

**First stage [D1] (here-and-now).** Before demand scenarios are revealed, choose hub sea repositioning arrivals/departures \(y_{i,t}^{\mathrm{in}}\) and \(y_{i,t}^{\mathrm{out}}\) for each hub \(i\) and period \(t\) in the window. Minimize sea cost plus the expected second-stage cost. Capacity and minimum export commitments couple \(y\) to vessel calls \(V_{i,t}\) and per-call bounds \(B^{\mathrm{in/out}}\), \(D^{\mathrm{ext,eff}}\).

**Second stage [D2] (wait-and-see).** For each scenario \(\omega\) with probability \(\pi_\omega\), after \(\eta^\omega\) is revealed, optimize inland repositioning flows \(x_{ij\kappa,t}^\omega\) on allowed arcs (road/rail with transit times \(\tau\)), inventories \(I_{n,t}^\omega\), leases \(r_{n,t}^\omega\), and spill \(s_{n,t}^\omega\). Inventory balance at hubs includes \(+y^{\mathrm{in}}-y^{\mathrm{out}}\); spokes include exogenous top-up \(E_{n,t}\); dry ports are inland only. Respect storage caps \(U_n\) and arc caps \(F_{ij\kappa}\). Spill is heavily penalized so that a feasible plan preferably meets demand without dumping inventory.

**Objective.** Minimize
\[
\sum_{t}\sum_{i\in\mathrm{hubs}}\bigl(c^{\mathrm{sea,in}} y_{i,t}^{\mathrm{in}}+c^{\mathrm{sea,out}} y_{i,t}^{\mathrm{out}}\bigr)
+\sum_{\omega}\pi_\omega\sum_{t}\Bigl(
\sum_{(i,j,\kappa)} c_{ij\kappa}^{\mathrm{land}} x_{ij\kappa,t}^\omega
+\sum_n c_n^{\mathrm{hold}} I_{n,t}^\omega
+\sum_n c_n^{\mathrm{lease}} r_{n,t}^\omega
+\sum_n c^{\mathrm{spill}} s_{n,t}^\omega\Bigr).
\]

Use the numeric sets and parameters in the provided data file (nodes, arcs, \(\xi\), scenario \(\eta^\omega\), costs, capacities). Implement the extensive-form DEP in Gurobi and report the optimal objective value.
