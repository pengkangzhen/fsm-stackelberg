A liner operator must reposition empty containers under **export-demand
uncertainty** on a sea–land network of hub seaports, spoke seaports, and dry
ports. There are **no** shipper or consignee street nodes. Empty supply is an
**exogenous deterministic** parameter; randomness appears only in
scenario-dependent export demand.

**Task.** For one planning window, formulate and solve a **two-stage
stochastic linear program** as a single extensive-form deterministic
equivalent (DEP). All decision variables are continuous nonnegative TEUs.

- **Stage 1 (here-and-now):** before demand scenarios are revealed, choose hub
  sea repositioning arrivals and departures. These decisions are
  non-anticipative and couple to vessel calls and per-call capacity / export
  floors (details in domain knowledge).
- **Stage 2 (wait-and-see):** for each demand scenario, optimize inland
  repositioning on allowed road/rail arcs (with transit times), inventories,
  leases, and spill. Spill is heavily penalized so that a good plan preferably
  meets demand without dumping inventory.

**Objective (categories).** Minimize sea repositioning cost plus the expected
second-stage cost (inland repositioning + holding + leasing + spill). Exact
symbols, constraints, and inventory balances are provided as optional domain
knowledge modules—request them by name from the catalog when needed.

**Data and output.** Use the numeric sets and parameters in the provided data
file. Implement the DEP in Gurobi and report the optimal objective value.
